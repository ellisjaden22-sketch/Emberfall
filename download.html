Installing the game app

Windows: unzip, then double-click Emberfall.exe. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (first time only).
Mac: unzip, then right-click Emberfall.app → Open → Open. If it's still blocked: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
The game connects to this realm automatically. Your heroes are saved on your own computer.
Controls: WASD move · mouse look · 1–8 abilities · F talk/loot · R mount · M map · Esc menu.
