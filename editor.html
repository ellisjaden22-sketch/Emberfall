The editor is building the map exactly the way the game does. This takes a few seconds.

How it works

1. Paint tiles, place props, NPCs, monster spawns and landmarks on the map.
2. Press Preview in game to walk around your changes right away (only you see the preview).
3. Press Export world.js and upload that file to your GitHub repository, next to index.html. After Render redeploys, everyone gets your changes.

Mouse: left click / drag uses the tool, right or middle drag moves the map, the wheel zooms.
Keys: R rotate the prop, [ ] brush size, Del delete the selected object, Ctrl+Z undo, Esc back to Select.

Your work is saved in this browser automatically. Export it whenever you are happy with it.
