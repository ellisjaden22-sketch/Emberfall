EMBERFALL ONLINE (BETA) - Mac

1. Drag Emberfall.app into your Applications folder (optional).
2. The first time: right-click Emberfall.app and choose Open, then Open again.
   If macOS still blocks it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway".
3. The game opens in its own window (uses Google Chrome, Edge or Brave if installed, otherwise your browser).
4. Create your own hero. Your characters are saved on this Mac.
