EMBERFALL ONLINE (BETA) - Windows

1. Double-click Emberfall.exe.
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the game is new and unsigned:
   click "More info", then "Run anyway". You only need to do this once.
2. The game opens in its own window. If the realm was asleep, it takes up to a minute to wake up.
3. Create your own hero. Your characters are saved on this PC.

Controls: WASD move, mouse to look, 1-8 abilities, F talk/loot, R mount, M map, C character,
B bags, P party, Esc menu (options, keybinds, sound, bug reports). Alt+F4 or the X closes the game.
Needs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome installed (every Windows 10/11 PC has Edge).
