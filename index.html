How to play

Move with WASD (A/D strafe). Drag with the left mouse button to turn the camera, drag with the right button to turn your character too, hold both buttons to run, scroll to zoom, Space to jump. Target enemies with Tab or a click; click another player or a party frame to target a friend. Abilities are on 1–6 (new ones unlock at levels 2, 3, 4 and 6), your potion is Q. Using an attack also starts auto-attacking. Moving interrupts spells that have a cast time. Red and orange circles on the ground are incoming attacks: step out before they fill.
The open world: people with ! over their heads have quests, and there are camps in every zone. Press M for the world map and L for your quest log. Glowing herbs and ore veins can be gathered with F; Mira brews them into potions and elixirs, Doran uses ore to upgrade your gear. Buy a mount from Hilde and press R to ride. You can swim in lakes. Try /dance, /wave, /sit (or X). Discover all the landmarks for bonus experience, and watch out for storms.
Frostpeak & Hollowdeep Crypt (level 10–15): ride east through the mountain pass past the Ruins of Vael to reach the frozen frontier. Jarl Hakon's quests lead to the Frost Giant and into the crypt, where Morvath the Lich waits. New abilities unlock at levels 8 and 11 (keys 7 and 8). Look up at night in Frostpeak.
Hearthmere is the safe town. Talk to Captain Oriel for quests and Mira the Peddler to buy potions and sell loot (stand close and press F, or click them).
Playing together: everyone with this page open is in the same world. Monsters, their health and their attacks are shared. The first player to hit a monster tags it. Join the same party (/party name or the Party button) to share kill credit, quest progress and party chat (/p). Menders can heal anyone.
Zones: Amberfield (lvl 1–2) · Thornwood (2–4) · Gloamfen (3–5) · Ruins of Vael (5–7) · Cinder Caldera (7–10, home of the Cinder Wyrm). Elites (★) and bosses (☠) hit hard and drop better loot. Day turns to night every 10 minutes. Your character is saved in this browser.
Mounts: from level 10, visit Hilde the Stable Master at the stables in Hearthmere (golden horseshoe 🐎 over her head and on your map). Press R to ride.
Loot: defeated monsters drop a glowing bag. Walk up and press F to open it, then click items or Take all. Item colours: Common · Uncommon · Rare · Epic · Legendary. Each boss shows its drop chances; the last bosses can drop your class's legendary weapon. In your bags, drag items to move, equip, sell or destroy them (or right-click).
Parties: click a player and press Invite to party, use the P window, or type /invite name. Party members share kill credit and quest progress; /p message talks to your party.
Game Masters: the owner of the game is a GM and sees an orange GM button. Type /gm for the GM tools: gold, levels, god mode, no cooldowns, noclip, speed, teleport, items, time, weather, kill and server announcements. Other players get "You are not a Game Master."
